Achieve anything, one card at a time.

Mandalai puts your goal in the middle of a 3×3 chart and the eight steps toward it around it. Any step can become the centre of its own chart, until every card is something you can simply do. Stuck? AI suggests the steps for you to keep, change or drop.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone

How it works

Write the goal. Break it into up to eight subgoals. Break those down too, as deep as you need. Tick the small things off and watch the big one fill in.

Without an account, your goals stay on your phone. Sign in to sync them, and to get free tokens for AI suggestions.
